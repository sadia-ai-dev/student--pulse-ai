# 🎓 Student Pulse

## 📌 About the App
Student Pulse is an AI-powered web application designed to support students in managing their academic stress, tracking their mood, and improving productivity. Many students struggle with stress, lack of focus, and poor study habits. This app provides a simple and smart solution by combining mood tracking with AI-based guidance.

It is specially built for students who want to stay motivated, organized, and mentally balanced during their studies.

## ✨ Features
- 😊 Mood Tracker – Track your daily mood and emotions  
- 🤖 AI Chat Assistant – Get instant help, motivation, and study advice  
- 📚 Study Tips Generator – Smart suggestions for better study habits  
- 💡 Stress Detection – Helps identify stress levels and gives solutions  
- ⚡ Clean UI – Simple and user-friendly interface  

---

## 🤖 AI Feature
The app includes an AI-powered assistant that interacts with students and provides personalized responses based on their mood and needs.

### 🔹 What it does:
- Suggests study techniques  
- Motivates students  
- Helps reduce stress  
- Gives productivity tips  

### 🔹 System Prompt:
You are Student Pulse AI, a helpful and intelligent AI assistant for students.
Your role is to help students with their academic tasks, study planning, learning problems, and productivity.
Provide clear, accurate, and easy-to-understand answers.
Guide students in a friendly and supportive way.
Help users create study schedules, understand concepts, and improve their learning habits.
Always keep responses relevant, respectful, and student-focused.
